Every 2 seconds, someone in an emergency room desperately needs blood.

Most of us have seen the panicked Facebook posts at 2:00 AM:  
*"URGENT: 2 bags of O-negative needed at DMCH within 3 hours. Please share!"*

Comments get cluttered, phone numbers get scraped by spammers, precious minutes tick away, and the patient's family is left pacing hospital corridors in total helplessness.

Back when I was completing my B.Sc. in CSE, sitting in our Mobile Application Lab, a thought struck me: **Why are we still relying on chaotic social media comments and unverified forwarding chains when our phones have GPS, real-time sync, and smart notifications?**

I built a raw, vague prototype for my semester lab project. It was rough around the edges, but the core heartbeat was there.

Then reality hit.  
After the semester ended, the code sat buried in a private GitHub repository for nearly 8 months, gathering digital dust.

When our final year defense arrived, I had a choice: pursue a traditional thesis or turn this project into something real. I pitched it to my supervisor with all the excitement in the world—only to be met with lukewarm enthusiasm and zero motivation. Disheartened, I shelved the project. It got buried again.

**Fast forward to today.**  
In this new era of AI and relentless building, I couldn't let the idea die. I picked the codebase back up, rewrote the architecture from the ground up, and gave it everything it deserved.

Meet **RedCellNet** 🩸 — an open, zero-cost emergency blood lifeline designed to replace panic with precision.

---

### What RedCellNet Does (And How It Works for Real People):

🎯 **1. Instant Emergency SOS Radar**  
When a patient needs blood, a request is dispatched immediately to compatible, ready donors in that city—matching ABO/Rh medical rules so universal plasma and platelet constraints are handled properly.

📍 **2. Mutual-Consent Live Donor Route Tracking**  
The hardest part for an anxious family in the hospital is the silence: *"Is the donor actually on the way?"*  
Once a donor accepts, with mutual consent, the receiver can see the donor's live approaching route, distance (km), and real-time hospital ETA. Complete peace of mind when seconds count.

🛡️ **3. Verified Donor Shield & Digital Pass**  
Donors can verify their blood group via genuine diagnostic lab reports to receive an official **"Verified by RedCellNet"** badge. Donors get an interactive, flippable Digital Pass card with their hospital QR, donation milestones, and WHO 90-day recovery cooldown counter.

📴 **4. Zero-Internet Offline Emergency Directory**  
Emergencies don't wait for good mobile data. RedCellNet caches regional certified blood banks and emergency contacts locally so users can call for help even offline.

🩺 **5. Personal Hemoglobin & Wellness Journal**  
Donors can log their Hb levels and vitals, keeping their health first while saving others.

📲 **6. Instant Android Home Screen Widgets**  
One-tap access to your donor pass, blood group readiness badge, and emergency SOS radar right from your phone’s home screen.
